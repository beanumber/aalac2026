Pre-workshop Homework

  • Watch:
    “y code when ai?”
    Hadley Wickham, Chief Scientist, Posit
    (~ 45 minutes): In this webinar, Hadley Wickham illustrates how he uses AI to do most of his actual coding work these days.
  • Read:
    • In the popular press:
      • Stanford HCAI (2023): Review of an AI Summit at Stanford.
      • Walsh (2025): An investigate piece from New York Magazine detailing the use of generative AI tools by college students.
      • Shirky (2025): An editorial by the vice provost at NYU advocating a return to oral and in-person assessment methods.
      • The Oberlin Luddite Club et al. (2025): An editorial written by students at Oberlin College asking their president to “terminate the College’s contract with Google Gemini and OpenAI”.
    • Scholarly papers:
      • ✴️ DeLuca et al. (2025): An article showing that while generative AI is able to complete statistics assignments to a reasonable degree, there are detectable differences in the use of language across different LLMs and groups of people.
        Note: Sunday’s plenary speaker Alex Reinhart is a co-author on this paper!
      • Daniotti et al. (2026): An article in Science showing that while generative AI tools help more experienced programmers improve their productivity, the same gains are not observed among entry-level developers.
      • Bien and Mukherjee (2025): A reflection on having students code with GitHub Copilot in a data science MBA course.
      • Fernandes et al. (2025): A study showing that while performance on the LSAT improved with AI use, metacognitive accuracy declined.

There are many, many more things to read on our reading list!
