Reading

We have collected the following non-exhaustive list of articles about generative AI and education. There are grouped roughly by topic and ordered by first author.

A 📌 emoji designates an article that has been recommended by a member of the organizing committee as particularly relevant reading for this group.

Suggestions for additions welcomed at https://github.com/beanumber/aalac2026/issues.
